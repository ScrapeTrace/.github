# ScrapeTrace

**Independent Anti-Bot Research Lab**

Tracing what happens when the web fights back.

We investigate anti-bot systems, browser identity, and the difficult parts of automated web access and extraction. Anti-bot research is our core, with connected work on scraping vendors, infrastructure, and extraction challenges.

## Inside the lab

- **[Anti-Bot](https://scrapetrace.com/anti-bot/):** browser and network fingerprints, automation artifacts, detection systems, and reverse engineering.
- **[Evaluations](https://scrapetrace.com/evaluations/):** evidence-based analysis of scraping providers, browser behavior, infrastructure, and reliability.
- **[Extraction Challenges](https://scrapetrace.com/extraction-challenges/):** font obfuscation, hostile content, crawler traps, rendering, and extraction reliability.
- **[Projects](https://scrapetrace.com/projects/):** tools and experiments that make signals and extraction problems easier to inspect.

## How we work

Follow the signal. Understand the system.

Experiments, evidence, reproducible methods, and transparent limitations guide the publication. We document test conditions, separate observations from explanations, and explain what each result can and cannot establish. Published work discloses relevant commercial relationships and welcomes corrections.

Read our [methodology and editorial principles](https://scrapetrace.com/methodology/).
